# ocrp

**o**ld · **c**lean · **r**ead · **p**aper — a small PDF reader I keep for myself: drop in a paper, select a passage, get the translation or ask about it, keep the note, and click a citation to see it without losing my place. Made for going through the literature fast, and for the times when I would rather slow down and read a good paper closely, the old way. It runs on the keyboard; press `?` for the keys.

**Live: https://jieqianghe.github.io/ocrp/**
